Display and statistical scope
These public-domain outlines replace no SPOT statistical values. They are
approximate display matches to the SPOT reporting entities, not the model's
original GADM municipal or national geometry and not a new spatial allocation.
Several map units may show the same reporting entity's national estimate.
Those repeated display values must not be added across polygons.

Natural Earth uses de facto cartographic boundaries. Names and outlines do not
imply agreement on territorial status. The explicit SPOT crosswalk preserves
the supplied reporting entities, including their package-specific identifiers.
Small islands can remain hard to see at world scale; all reporting entities
remain available through search and the complete data table.

SPOT data retain their separate release attribution and licence. The
application's software licence does not change these source notices.
