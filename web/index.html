What the atlas shows

SPOT follows municipal waste and plastic through generation, collection, recovery, disposal and emissions. National estimates aggregate municipality-level calculations. A reporting entity may be a country or a territory.

Mean, median and uncertainty

The mean is the average of the modelled draws. The median is their midpoint. The 5th–95th percentile range contains the central 90% of the modelled values. This range describes uncertainty under the model’s assumptions; it does not establish predictive accuracy.

The flow and source diagrams use mean masses so additive pathways remain consistent. Marginal medians and percentiles do not generally add up across flows or countries. Regional intervals cannot be calculated by summing country percentiles.

Plastic emissions, in two forms

Plastic debris emissions are plastic released to the environment. Openly burned plastic is reported as a plastic mass. Neither is the same quantity as the mass of an individual air pollutant. The five source categories are litter, uncollected waste, collection, disposal and recycling.

Different denominators, different questions

Collection service coverage is a percentage of municipal waste generation excluding uncollected litter. It differs from the share of all generated waste that is collected and delivered. Per-person plastic emissions are kilograms per person per year; waste-generation rates are kilograms per person per day.

What a missing value means

Unavailable or undefined values remain blank or are marked “Unavailable”. An explicit zero remains zero. Coefficients with a zero denominator can be undefined; they are not automatically 0%.

Dates and scope

2020 is the result year, separate from the 2026 source release. These results do not supply an annual trend or a transport simulation of plastic through rivers and oceans. This atlas serves the published baseline; it does not rerun the model when you change a selection.
